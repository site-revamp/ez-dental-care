# EZ Dental Care --- Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

EZ Dental Care is a neighbourhood dental practice serving adults,
children, and families across its Bedok North and Geylang Bahru clinics
in Singapore. The website helps patients understand the practice's
dental services and find the right clinic for enquiries and
appointments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on clearer clinic selection, easier
discovery of treatments, and more direct access to treatment pricing and
subsidy information.

## Main Improvements

1.  **Clearer clinic-specific booking actions**
    -   **Previous issue:** The main booking button opened the Bedok
        North WhatsApp chat without clearly identifying the branch,
        which could misdirect visitors looking for Geylang Bahru.
    -   **Change:** The hero section now guides visitors to choose a
        branch. The mobile action bar provides separate, labelled
        actions: **WhatsApp Bedok** and **Call Geylang**.
2.  **Simplified treatment navigation**
    -   **Previous issue:** The homepage displayed 15 treatment entries,
        including two links to the same scaling and polishing page,
        making the list harder to scan.
    -   **Change:** Treatments are now organised into six groups based
        on common patient needs. Each group links to the relevant
        existing treatment pages, and the groups display in two columns
        on mobile screens.
3.  **More visible treatment fees and subsidies**
    -   **Previous issue:** Rates were accessible through the Services
        menu and footer, while cost guidance appeared farther down in
        the FAQ. The main treatment section did not provide a direct
        route to this information.
    -   **Change:** A **View treatment rates** link is now available in
        the hero, followed by a **Fees & subsidies** section after the
        treatment groups with direct links to rates and CHAS
        information.

## Design Improvements

-   **Homepage structure:** The hero, treatment groups, and fees section
    create a more direct path from choosing a clinic to exploring care
    options and checking costs.
-   **Navigation and usability:** Branch-specific actions make the
    intended clinic clearer, while grouped treatments reduce the amount
    of scanning required.
-   **Responsive layout:** Treatment groups use a two-column layout on
    phones, and the mobile action bar keeps the two clinic actions
    distinct.
-   **Information hierarchy:** Treatment rates and subsidy information
    are surfaced closer to the treatment content instead of relying only
    on menu, footer, or FAQ navigation.
